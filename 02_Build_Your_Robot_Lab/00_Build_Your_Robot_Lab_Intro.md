# Build Your Robot Lab

In this session, you will learn how to transition from theory to physical execution by assembling your hardware, standardizing your task environment, and establishing your control loops. Building an Embodied AI pipeline requires strict kinematic and visual consistency between the digital twin (simulation) and the physical world.

Specifically, you will learn how to unbox and assemble the **Seeed reBot Arm B601**, reconfigure its motor IDs, and perform critical **joint and hand-eye calibrations** to ensure precise real-world tracking. You will then construct a standardized light-controlled workspace (the Lightbox) to match the visual assets in Isaac Lab. Finally, you will master **two distinct operational modalities**: executing direct hardware teleoperation for real-world data collection, and deploying a seamless **Real-to-Sim-to-Real loop**.
